# Apex Dental Care: Clinical Governance, Cancellation & Patient Rights Policy

Apex Dental Care is committed to delivering world-class, sterile, and ethical dental health services across all metropolitan centers. To ensure that our operating rooms maintain optimal clinical scheduling and that emergency cases receive rapid intervention, all registered patients are requested to observe our unified appointment protocol.

### 1. Appointment Scheduling and Confirmation Protocol
Every scheduled procedure is reserved exclusively for the patient alongside a sterilized instrument tray and a dedicated dental operatory. Patients receive automated SMS and WhatsApp confirmation links twenty-four hours before their visit. We request patients to confirm their attendance at least six hours prior to their arrival time. If an appointment remains unconfirmed three hours prior to the slot, our central reception will contact the patient by telephone to verify attendance.

### 2. Rescheduling and Cancellation Guidelines
We understand that personal emergencies and urgent work commitments arise unexpectedly. Routine dental checkups, cleaning sessions, and orthodontic adjustments may be rescheduled or cancelled up to two hours prior to the appointment without any cancellation surcharge or forfeit of registration deposits. Cancellations requested less than one hour before the designated time or outright patient no-shows for specialized surgical procedures may incur a nominal rescheduling fee of ₹500 on their subsequent visit.

### 3. Emergency Prioritization and Clinical Delay Disclosures
Our clinics provide emergency trauma intervention for acute pulpitis, facial trauma, avulsed teeth, and severe hemorrhage. In rare instances where an emergency patient arrives in acute pain, our attending oral surgeon may prioritize their immediate stabilization. Should this cause a slight delay in your elective procedure, our clinical floor manager will personally brief you within ten minutes and offer you the choice of priority rescheduling or a comfortable lounge wait with complimentary refreshments.

### 4. Sterilization and Infection Control Standards
Every Apex Dental clinic complies strictly with the European Class-B autoclaving standards and single-use consumable guidelines. All rotary handpieces, surgical burs, and ultrasonic scaling tips undergo vacuum autoclave sterilization at 134 degrees Celsius before every clinical session. Patients are welcome to request that sterile instrument pouches be unsealed directly in their presence inside the operatory.

### 5. Patient Rights and Financial Transparency
Every patient possesses the inviolable right to receive a written, itemized treatment estimate before any procedure is initiated. Our doctors are strictly prohibited from performing unapproved exploratory procedures or recommending unnecessary radiographic investigations. All diagnostic digital X-rays and CBCT scans remain the medical property of the patient and are delivered securely via email upon request at no additional fee.
