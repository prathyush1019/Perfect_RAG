### Question 1: How do I book an emergency dental appointment at Apex Dental Care?
Answer: You can book an immediate emergency slot by calling our 24/7 centralized helpline at +91 80 4123 9801 or walking straight into our Anna Nagar or Indiranagar trauma centers. Same-day emergency appointments are prioritized within 30 minutes of arrival.

### Question 2: What should I do if a dental crown becomes loose or falls off?
Answer: Do not attempt to re-affix the crown using commercial adhesives or glue. Place the crown in a clean, sealed container, avoid chewing on that side of your mouth, and visit any Apex clinic within 24 hours so our dentists can inspect the abutment and re-cement it securely.

### Question 3: Is the laser teeth whitening procedure painful or damaging to tooth enamel?
Answer: No. Our laser teeth whitening utilizes cool-wavelength dental lasers with gentle hydrogen peroxide gels that do not strip enamel minerals or harm pulp vitality. Most patients experience zero discomfort, while a minority may feel mild temporary sensitivity for 4 to 6 hours.

### Question 4: How long does a single sitting root canal treatment take?
Answer: Thanks to our advanced rotary endodontic systems and digital apex locators, a standard single-sitting root canal procedure is typically completed in 45 to 60 minutes under local anesthesia.

### Question 5: Do you accept dental insurance policies and third-party healthcare financing?
Answer: Yes. We partner with all leading health insurance providers for inpatient oral surgery coverage, and provide zero-interest Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) plans for cosmetic, implant, and aligner procedures across 3 to 12 months.

### Question 6: What is the warranty policy on Zirconia dental crowns?
Answer: Every Zirconia Monolithic Crown installed at Apex Dental Care carries a 15-year replacement warranty covering structural fractures, material chipping, or marginal discrepancies, accompanied by a verified authenticity certificate.

### Question 7: How often should I schedule ultrasonic dental scaling and polishing?
Answer: We recommend ultrasonic scaling once every six months to prevent calculus buildup, halt gingivitis progression, and maintain oral hygiene. Patients with active periodontal issues may require visits every 3 to 4 months.

### Question 8: Can I eat immediately after receiving a tooth-colored composite filling?
Answer: Yes. Composite resin fillings are completely light-cured and hardened instantly in the dental operatory, so you may eat immediately once the local numbness subsides. We recommend avoiding scalding hot beverages for two hours.

### Question 9: What are the primary prerequisites before starting clear orthodontic aligner therapy?
Answer: Before aligners can be fabricated, a patient must have healthy gums, zero untreated cavities, and complete a high-resolution 3D intraoral scan alongside panoramic digital radiography.

### Question 10: What is your policy regarding appointment cancellations and rescheduling?
Answer: Patients may cancel or reschedule their dental appointments without penalty up to 2 hours prior to their scheduled slot by calling our branch reception or using our automated WhatsApp booking assistant.
